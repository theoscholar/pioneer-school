The Joy of Jehovah Is Your Stronghold

Prep notes from "Fully Accomplish Your Ministry" (pt14), Insight Vol. 2, and the New World Translation.

  • Model answer = a starting point. Say it your own way when you comment.

Opening Discussion · joy that gives strength

Heart of it: The joy Jehovah gives is an unfailing stronghold — a source of strength for those walking in his way. It's not a bonus; it's fuel for our service.

In Ezra's day, when the people understood God's Law, they were told: "do not feel sad, for the joy of Jehovah is your stronghold." They applied what they learned, held a joyous festival, and gained strength for worship. We have every reason to be joyful too.

Nehemiah 8:10 — "Do not feel sad, for the joy of Jehovah is your stronghold."

🔍Look it up: Prov. 2:6-8·Neh. 8:1-18

"Nothing but Joyful" · where joy comes from

Heart of it: True joy isn't a mood that comes and goes — it's a fruit of God's spirit, rooted in our relationship with him.

QWhat is the source of true joy, and how is God's spirit involved?

Joy is a fruitage of the spirit — so it grows as we let God's spirit work in us. Our close relationship with Jehovah, and the blessed understanding of his Word, make us the most joyful people on earth.

🔍Look it up: Gal. 5:22·Prov. 4:18

QWhy can it be said that joy is not optional?

Because it's part of the fruitage of the spirit that God expects to see in us — "Always rejoice." Cultivating joy is part of faithful worship, not a nice extra.

Philippians 4:4 — "Always rejoice in the Lord. Again I will say, Rejoice!"

🔍Look it up: Rom. 5:8, 18·John 8:32

A Life of Purpose & a Blessed Brotherhood · joy shared

Heart of it: Meaningful work and a worldwide family of friends are two of the greatest sources of joy in a pioneer's life.

QWhy is our ministry a source of such joy?

It's the most important work on earth — and teaching others engraves the truths deeper on our own hearts. Our work brings joy to Jehovah, to the angels, and to those who hear. Jehovah wants us to enjoy our work.

🔍Look it up: Luke 15:10·Acts 20:35·Eccl. 5:19

QHow does our brotherhood bring joy — and how can we add to others' joy?

Our international family gives us "a hundredfold" of brothers and sisters — how good it is to dwell together in unity! And pioneers who encourage and stabilize others find that "the generous person will prosper."

Proverbs 11:25 — "The generous person will prosper, and whoever refreshes others will himself be refreshed."

🔍Look it up: Ps. 133:1·Mark 10:29, 30

An Unfailing Stronghold · joy through hard times

Heart of it: Joy doesn't erase our problems — but it gives us the strength to endure them with a positive spirit.

QWhy is a positive spirit important, since joy doesn't remove difficulties?

Like Jesus, who "for the joy that was set before him endured," a joyful outlook helps us bear up under trials. Being assured of Jehovah's love — he is "close to the brokenhearted" — steadies us.

🔍Look it up: Heb. 12:2·Jas. 1:2·Ps. 34:18

QHow is the joy of Jehovah an unfailing stronghold?

Knowing what Jehovah has done, is doing, and will do fills us with joy and gratitude — a strength that outlasts every hardship. "This is our God… let us be joyful and rejoice in the salvation by him."

🔍Look it up: Isa. 25:9·Col. 1:9-11

Say it your way: Name one thing about your service to Jehovah that brings you deep joy — and share why.
