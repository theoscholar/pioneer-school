Opening Discussion · a gift no one can take

Heart of it: Prayer is a priceless gift. Opposers might take your Bible or stop your meetings, but no one can rob you of prayer. It's impossible to overstate its value.

The Bible isn't a prayer book, yet it's the greatest textbook on prayer — the Hebrew Scriptures alone hold over 150 prayers, from kings and captives, in triumph and trouble. As pioneers, your full reliance on Jehovah in prayer will carry you through life's challenges.

Psalm 65:2 — "O Hearer of prayer, to you people of all sorts will come."

🔍Look it up: Ps. 141:2·Eph. 6:18

Learn From Notable Prayers · Jesus and Hannah

Heart of it: The Bible's great prayers teach us how to pray — with dependence on God, and by pouring out our specific concerns.

QWhat do we learn from Jesus' prayers?

Though perfect, Jesus didn't rely on his own strength — he prayed intensely (even all night before choosing the apostles) and submitted, "let… your will take place" (Matt. 26:42). "Keep on asking, and it will be given you."

Matthew 7:7 — "Keep on asking, and it will be given you; keep on seeking, and you will find."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 26:39, 42·Luke 6:12, 13·Heb. 5:7

QWhat does Hannah teach us about specific, heartfelt prayer?

Facing deep grief, Hannah poured out her heart and named her exact difficulty — and afterward "her face was no longer downcast" (1 Sam. 1:18). Being specific unburdens the heart. Whatever the trouble — health, loneliness, family — describe it to Jehovah and how you feel.

🔍Look it up: 1 Sam. 1:10, 11·1 Sam. 1:18·Ps. 62:8

Say it your way: What's one thing you'd like to pray about more specifically? (Share only what you're comfortable with.)
Work in Harmony With Your Prayers · faith plus action

Heart of it: We don't just pray and wait — we act in line with what we ask for.

QHow did faithful ones work in harmony with their prayers?

Nehemiah prayed and posted a guard. Jesus prayed for more workers, then sent out the disciples. When we pray for wisdom or for the ministry, we then do our part — and pray with full confidence.

🔍Look it up: Neh. 4:9·Matt. 9:37, 38·Jas. 1:5

QWhy pray with confidence in Jehovah?

Because "Jehovah is near to all those calling on him." He hears, he cares, and "the eyes of Jehovah are on the righteous" (Ps. 34:15). What a privilege to have the Creator of the universe close to us!

Psalm 145:18 — "Jehovah is near to all those calling on him, to all who call on him in truth."

🔍Look it up: Heb. 11:6·1 John 5:14

Say it your way: Name one way you could "work in harmony" with a prayer you've been praying.
