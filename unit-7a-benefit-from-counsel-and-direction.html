Opening Discussion · a precious gift

Heart of it: Elders are a gift from Jesus to care for us. Cooperating with them — and with the faithful slave — is really cooperating with Christ, the Head of the congregation.

When Jesus ascended, "he gave gifts in men" — overseers appointed by holy spirit to shepherd us with tenderness. These men are in Christ's right hand, under his direction, so they deserve our full cooperation and respect.

Ephesians 4:8 — "When he ascended on high… he gave gifts in men."

🔍Look it up: Acts 20:28·1 Thess. 5:12, 13

Working Submissively With "Gifts in Men" · why cooperate

Heart of it: Jesus guides us through faithful undershepherds. Submitting to them isn't weakness — it's how we follow Christ.

QTo whom did Jesus delegate oversight, and who is the faithful and discreet slave?

Jesus entrusted the flock's care to appointed men. The "faithful and discreet slave" (Matt. 24:45) is the small group of anointed brothers (the Governing Body) who provide spiritual food at the proper time.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 24:45-47·John 21:15-17·w13 7/15 pp. 21-23

QWhy should pioneers be obedient and submissive to overseers?

Because "gifts in men" (Eph. 4:8) were given to build us up and keep us stable. Being obedient makes their work a joy, "for this would be damaging to you" otherwise.

Hebrews 13:17 — "Be obedient to those who are taking the lead among you and be submissive… so that they may do this with joy and not with sighing."

🔍Look it up: Heb. 13:7·Eph. 4:11-14

Shepherds Keep Watch — Receiving Counsel · how we respond matters

Heart of it: Elders lose sleep watching over us. And how we take counsel reveals a lot — humble hearts grow "even more desirable in God's sight."

QHow do loving shepherds keep watch over the flock?

The word for "keeping watch" literally means "abstaining from sleep." Elders may lose sleep out of concern for us — imitating the tender care of Jesus.

🔍Look it up: Gal. 6:1·1 Pet. 5:2, 3

QWhy do we all need counsel — and how should we react to it?

"We all stumble many times" (James 3:2), so counsel is a kindness. We resist the urge to justify ourselves or question the counselor's motive; instead we listen and adjust, unlike Saul, who made excuses and lost his kingship.

🔍Look it up: Jas. 3:2·Prov. 19:20·1 Sam. 15:24-28

Say it your way: Recall a time counsel helped you. How did responding well bless you? That's a warm comment.
Support the Circuit Overseer & Provisions for Pioneers · make the most of it

Heart of it: The circuit overseer's visit and the special pioneer provisions are gifts to strengthen you — dive in fully.

QHow does the circuit overseer's visit provide an "interchange of encouragement" (Rom. 1:12)?

Both give and receive — "iron sharpens iron" (Prov. 27:17). Support the field service arrangements, and if you can, join the overseer or his wife in the ministry or on a Bible study.

🔍Look it up: Rom. 1:11, 12·Prov. 27:17

QHow can pioneers get the most from the spiritual provisions arranged for them?

By attending and applying the pioneer meetings (during the C.O. visit, the annual meeting with elders, the circuit assembly meeting, and this school) — and like Mary, 'choosing the good portion.'

🔍Look it up: Luke 10:38-42

Say it your way: Name one way the elders or a traveling overseer have helped you or your family — a lovely experience to share.
