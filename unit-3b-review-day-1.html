Review: Unit 1(b) · Fortify Your Relationship With Jehovah
Psalm 77:12 — "I will meditate on all your activity."

●Why, despite a busy pioneer routine, do we make time for study, meditation, and prayer?

Because these are the lifelines that keep our friendship with Jehovah strong — nothing matters more, so we protect the time.

●How does meditation lead to a more intimate relationship with God?

It moves truth from head to heart — reflecting on Jehovah's qualities and acts makes our love personal and deep.

●How do we benefit from reflecting on Jehovah's answers to our prayers?

Seeing him respond builds our confidence that he's real, near, and listening — which makes us pray all the more.

Review: Unit 2(a) · New World Translation, Part 1
2 Timothy 2:15 — "Handling the word of the truth aright."

●In what ways is the New World Translation superior?

It restores God's name, translates accurately from the best manuscripts, avoids doctrinal bias, and uses clear modern language.

●What principles guided the translation committee?

Convey the correct meaning (not just word-for-word), keep God's name, and stay faithful to the original text.

●What does the name Jehovah mean, and why are we sure it belongs in the Bible?

"He Causes to Become." The name appears about 7,000 times in the Hebrew text — removing it was tradition, not scholarship.

Review: Unit 2(b) · New World Translation, Part 2
Psalm 119:97 — "How I do love your law!"

●Name some appendix features — how will you use them?

Glossary, Bible Words Index, maps and charts, "Introduction to God's Word" — for study, meeting prep, and teaching others.

●What do you recall about footnotes and marginal references?

Footnotes give alternate/literal meanings ("Or," "Lit."); cross-references link parallel verses and fulfilled prophecy.

●How can you build enthusiasm for the New World Translation?

By showing people its features door to door and on studies — enthusiasm is contagious when you use the tool yourself.

Review: Unit 3(a) · Keep Pace With Spiritual Enlightenment
Luke 12:12 — "The holy spirit will teach you."

●How did true servants respond to enlightenment in the first century, and today?

Then and now: they trust the arrangement Jehovah is using, accept new light humbly, and move ahead united.

●Why is it important for pioneers to stay up-to-date — and how will you?

To avoid being "left behind." By keeping up with our publications, meetings, and jw.org, and researching under "Beliefs Clarified."

🔍Look it up: w10 7/15 p. 24, par. 15
