Review: Unit 7(a) · Benefit From Counsel and Direction
Galatians 6:1 — "Try to readjust such a man in a spirit of mildness."

●How does the way we accept counsel reflect our spirituality?

A humble, teachable response — not excuses — shows a spiritual heart and makes us "even more desirable in God's sight."

●How can pioneers cooperate with elders and traveling overseers?

By respecting them as "gifts in men," supporting their arrangements, and gladly working alongside them.

Review: Unit 7(b) · Resist "the Spirit of the World"
1 Corinthians 2:12 — "We received, not the spirit of the world, but the spirit that is from God."

●Why should Bible principles guide our entertainment, dress, and grooming?

Because these choices reveal what we love — and guard against the world's spirit taking root in the heart.

●How can you keep your life simple and help others resist that spirit?

By cultivating contentment, watching our associations, and setting a good example that encourages others.

Review: Unit 8(a) · Walk in the Way of Integrity
1 Chronicles 29:17 — "You take pleasure in integrity."

●What does it mean to walk in integrity, and what may it require?

Whole-hearted, blameless devotion to Jehovah — which may require neutrality and courage under trial or persecution.

●What's the connection between Bible principles and integrity?

Principles are the compass — applying God's Word (like "God's things to God") keeps our course straight.

Review: Unit 8(b) · Various Forms of Our Ministry
1 Corinthians 9:23 — "I do all things for the sake of the good news."

●How can you create opportunities to preach during daily activities?

By witnessing informally — to neighbors, workmates, and people you meet — starting friendly conversations wherever you go.

●How can you increase your share in various forms of ministry?

By trying public witnessing too — carts, transport, parks — and encouraging others to join in.

Review: Unit 9(a) · Workshop 2
Hebrews 13:15 — "Let us always offer to God a sacrifice of praise."

●How can you build confidence for informal witnessing, and why does it bring joy?

Start with common ground and no pressure; the joy comes from stretching yourself and reaching people you'd otherwise miss.
