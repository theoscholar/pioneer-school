Opening Discussion · from contact to Bible study

Heart of it: Earlier workshops built skills for starting conversations. This one focuses on successful return visits with the goal of starting Bible studies.

Building on what you've learned about showing personal interest and reaching all kinds of people, this session helps you turn initial interest into an ongoing study — remembering that "God kept making it grow."

1 Corinthians 3:6 — "I planted, Apollos watered, but God kept making it grow."
Focus 1 · A Positive Attitude · look forward to it

Heart of it: How we feel about return visits shows — and shapes the results. Approach each one expecting good.

✓Return when you promised.

Being reliable in "what is least" builds trust — and shows the person they matter to you.

🔍Look it up: Luke 16:10

Focus 2 · A Reasonable Objective · one step at a time

Heart of it: Have a clear, achievable goal for each visit — a small step toward starting a study.

✓Pick one aim per visit.

Answer a question, show a short video, or consider a paragraph — each step moves toward offering a Bible study.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 28:19, 20

Focus 3 · Prepare the Groundwork · set up next time

Heart of it: End each visit by planting a reason to come back.

✓Leave a question open.

"Next time I'll show you what the Bible says about…" — anticipation is a gentle, natural bridge to the next conversation.

Say it your way: After the demonstrations, jot one groundwork question you'll try on your next call.

Workshop prep ✅

  • Bring one return-visit experience — yours or one you've read.
  • Pick one skill: attitude, a clear objective, or laying groundwork.
  • Watch and borrow one good idea from the demonstrations.
