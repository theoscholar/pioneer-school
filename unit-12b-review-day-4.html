Review: Unit 10(a) · Appreciate Jesus' Role
Revelation 19:10 — "The witness concerning Jesus is what inspires prophecy."

●How does knowing Jesus' role motivate us to preach zealously?

He is "the way, the truth, and the life" — the one name that saves — so sharing him feels urgent and precious.

●How can we help others appreciate Jesus' role?

By showing how his ransom opens the way to Jehovah and to everlasting life.

Review: Unit 10(b) · Think in Terms of Bible Principles
Proverbs 4:7 — "Wisdom is the most important thing."

●How does thinking in principles make you a better pioneer?

It lets you make wise decisions even where no rule spells things out — and perceive Jehovah's will.

●Give an example of a Bible principle that improved your life.

Your own answer — pick one principle and say how applying it made a real difference.

Review: Unit 11(a) · Examine Your Spiritual Progress
1 Corinthians 9:24 — "Run in such a way that you may win it."

●What marks a spiritually-minded Christian, and how has self-analysis helped you?

Being God-oriented — thinking Jehovah's thoughts. Honest self-analysis reveals our motives and weaknesses so we can grow.

●How can we avoid comparing ourselves with others?

By measuring progress against God's standards and our own past, not against other people.

Review: Unit 11(b) & 12(a) · Make Effective Return Visits
1 Corinthians 3:6 — "I planted, Apollos watered, but God kept making it grow."

●Why are preparation and a positive attitude essential for return visits?

They turn a first chat into an ongoing conversation — and our attitude affects how the person responds.

●How can you lay the groundwork for starting a Bible study?

Plant an open question to answer next time, and feature a scripture on every visit so the purpose is clear.

Say it your way: Write one sentence for each unit above — ready comments for the review.

Review-day tip ✅
