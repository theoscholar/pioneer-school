Opening Discussion · the Bible pattern

Heart of it: Going to people in their homes isn't just a method we picked — it's the pattern Jesus set and the apostles followed.

Jesus told the apostles to "search out" (Matt. 10:11) deserving ones by going to their houses, taking the initiative to visit — no prior invitation needed. That's why house-to-house preaching still reaches people no other method can.

Acts 20:20 — Paul taught "publicly and from house to house."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 10:11-14·Luke 9:6

Jesus' Attitude Toward Preaching · his life's work

Heart of it: Preaching was the very center of Jesus' life — and reflecting on his zeal fuels ours.

QHow do Luke 4:43 and John 4:34 sum up Jesus' lifework, and what motivated him?

Jesus said he "must also declare the good news" (Luke 4:43) — doing his Father's will was like food to him. Love for Jehovah, and for people, drove him.

🔍Look it up: Luke 4:43·John 4:34·John 14:31·cf pp. 84-86

QBy what authority are we commissioned, and what does it involve?

By the risen Jesus, who has "all authority." The commission is to make disciples of people of all nations — teaching them everything he commanded.

Matthew 28:19, 20 — "Go, therefore, and make disciples of people of all the nations… teaching them to observe all the things I have commanded you."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 28:18-20·cf pp. 94-96

Jesus' Love and Compassion · how he saw people

Heart of it: Jesus looked at crowds and felt pity, not annoyance. Seeing people as he did makes us more patient and loving — even with those who reject us.

Matthew 9:36 — "he felt pity for them, because they were skinned and thrown about like sheep without a shepherd."

QHow did Jesus view people — and what if we find that hard?

He saw the good in them (as with Nathanael) and cared about the burdens weighing them down. If a positive view is hard, we can pray for Jehovah's own compassion — he felt distress over people's suffering (Isa. 63:9).

🔍Look it up: John 1:47·Isa. 63:9·Jonah 4:11

QWhat does the house-to-house work accomplish?

It gives everyone a chance to hear, makes us "clean from the blood of all" (Acts 20:26), warns of the coming judgment, and gathers those who love righteousness. If it feels hard, we can pray for boldness.

🔍Look it up: Acts 20:26, 27·2 Pet. 3:11-14·Luke 11:13

The Joys — Imitate Jesus · keep the fire burning

Heart of it: House-to-house work is still our principal method — and it brings real joy and blessings when we keep Jesus' example before us.

QWhy is house-to-house still our principal way — and why does it bring joy?

It reaches everyone, impartially, and gives a repeated witness. Like the 70 who "returned with joy" (Luke 10:17), we're thrilled to share good news and see the fruitage of the spirit grow in us.

🔍Look it up: kr p. 76, par. 22·Luke 10:17·Gal. 5:22, 23

QIf your zeal cools, how can Jesus' example spur you on — and why not let hostility stop you?

Jesus rose early and moved on to preach in "the towns nearby" (Mark 1:38) — recalling his drive reignites ours. And since he warned we'd face opposition, a hostile response is expected, never a reason to quit. "The one who has endured to the end will be saved" (Matt. 10:22).

🔍Look it up: Matt. 9:35-38·Mark 1:35-39·Matt. 10:22
