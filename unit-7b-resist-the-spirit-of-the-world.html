Opening Discussion · a spiritual battle

Heart of it: We're in a real fight — not with people, but against Satan's "spirit of the world." Our unbeatable defense is Jehovah's holy spirit.

The enemy is powerful and experienced, and his weapon — "the spirit of the world" — has conquered most of mankind. But we're not helpless. By availing ourselves of God's spirit and displaying its fruitage, we can win.

1 Corinthians 2:12 — "We received, not the spirit of the world, but the spirit that is from God."

🔍Look it up: Gal. 5:22, 23·Eph. 2:2, 3

What Is the Spirit of the World? · know your enemy

Heart of it: It's the attitude — the mental inclination — that pervades the world: selfishness, defiance of God, "do what I want." It works quietly on the heart.

QHow would you describe "the spirit of the world," and how does it exercise "authority"?

It's the dominant attitude of the world — independence from God, pride, and craving. It "exercises authority" by shaping how people think until it feels normal, "now at work" in those who defy God.

🔍Look it up: John 8:44·w12 10/15 p. 13, par. 4

QHow can it take root in a person's mind and heart?

Gradually and unnoticed — through what we take in. That's why we "safeguard the heart," because everything we do flows from it.

🔍Look it up: Prov. 4:23

Questions to Ponder · where it sneaks in

Heart of it: Four honest self-checks: our associations, our entertainment, our view of material things, and our dress and grooming.

1 Corinthians 15:33 — "Do not be misled. Bad associations spoil useful habits."

QHow do our associations — including online — affect us?

We become like those we're close to. Even social networking can pull us off course or affect our congregation privileges, so we're careful whom (and what) we let close.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 26:4·Prov. 22:1·Eph. 5:15, 16

QWhat do our entertainment and our view of material things reveal?

They show what we love. Satan uses unclean media to ensnare; choosing it would be hypocritical for those who "love Jehovah." And godly contentment — "having food and clothing" — guards us from the trap of materialism.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 97:10·Isa. 52:11·1 Tim. 6:8-10

QHow might dress and grooming reflect the world's spirit?

When someone treats personal "rights" in dress as more important than qualifying for privileges, the world's spirit is showing. We aim for modest, balanced choices that don't stumble others.

🔍Look it up: 1 Tim. 2:9, 10·Phil. 4:5

Say it your way: Pick one of the four areas above — what's one safeguard you personally keep? (Share only what you're comfortable with.)
Keep the Congregation Clean — Keep Fighting · love in action

Heart of it: Protecting the congregation's cleanness is an act of love — for Jehovah, for the congregation, and even for the wrongdoer. And the fight is winnable.

QIf you learn of serious wrongdoing, why not close your eyes to it?

Silence can share the guilt. Lovingly encourage the person to approach the elders; if they won't, bringing it to the elders shows love for Jehovah, the congregation, and the person — it opens the way to help and healing.

🔍Look it up: Lev. 5:1·Jas. 5:14, 15

QWhy keep up the fight to resist the spirit of the world?

Because Jesus already conquered the world — with Jehovah's spirit we can too. Keeping the fight going protects our friendship with God and our joy.

John 16:33 — "But take courage! I have conquered the world."

🔍Look it up: Gal. 5:16, 17·Jude 3

Say it your way: How does knowing Jesus already "conquered the world" strengthen you for this fight?
