Opening Discussion · part of the great issue

Heart of it: Satan claims people serve God only for selfish gain. Every day we keep our integrity, we help prove him a liar — and make Jehovah's heart rejoice.

Walking in integrity means loyal attachment to God even when it's hard — under trial, temptation, or opposition. When we hold firm, Jehovah can "make a reply to the one who taunts him." Like Job, we can resolve never to let our integrity go.

Job 27:5 — "Until I die, I will not renounce my integrity!"
Proverbs 27:11 — "Be wise, my son, and make my heart rejoice, so that I can make a reply to him who taunts me."

🔍Look it up: Job 1:9-11·1 Chron. 29:17

What It Means & Christian Neutrality · whose side we're on

Heart of it: Integrity is being whole-hearted and blameless toward God. A big part of that is neutrality — not taking sides in the world's conflicts and politics.

QWhat does "integrity" mean, and what does neutrality involve?

Integrity is being complete, whole-hearted, and blameless in devotion to Jehovah. Christian neutrality flows from accepting him as Ruler of our lives — so we don't share in wars, politics, or voting, and we honor no national symbols as objects of worship.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 119:1-3·Isa. 2:4·lff lesson 45

QHow would you explain your neutral stand, and why rely fully on Jehovah under trial?

Like Jesus, our "Kingdom is no part of this world," so we don't take sides. Under persecution we lean completely on Jehovah, confident he "knows how to rescue people of godly devotion" and won't let us be tested beyond what we can bear.

John 18:36 — "My Kingdom is no part of this world."

🔍Look it up: John 17:14, 16·1 Cor. 10:13·2 Pet. 2:9

Integrity Under Trial & Bible Principles · God first, then Caesar

Heart of it: Opposition can actually strengthen us. And Bible principles — not fear — guide how we relate to the government: we give Caesar his due, and God what belongs only to him.

Matthew 22:21 — "Pay back Caesar's things to Caesar, but God's things to God."

QWhat quality does persecution often build, and what preparations can we make now?

Endurance — trials produce a "tested quality" that leads to happiness and God's approval. We prepare now by strengthening our faith, our habits of prayer and study, and our trust that opponents will never truly prevail.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 5:10·Jas. 1:2-4·Isa. 54:17

QWhat do we owe Caesar — and what do we owe only to Jehovah?

We're honest, law-abiding citizens: we pay taxes, respect authority, and obey laws that don't conflict with God's. But our worship, our life, and our exclusive devotion belong to Jehovah alone — "we must obey God as ruler rather than men."

🔍Look it up: Rom. 13:1, 7·Titus 3:1, 2·Acts 5:29

Say it your way: How would you kindly explain your neutrality to a workmate or classmate? Draft it in one or two sentences.

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: what integrity means, how to explain neutrality, and giving God vs. Caesar their due.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Job 27:5 or Matthew 22:21.
  • Prepare now: think through how you'd reason on one situation the authorities might raise (a ban on preaching, a vote requirement, etc.).
