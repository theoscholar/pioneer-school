Opening Discussion · learn from the Master Teacher

Heart of it: Teaching is how we fulfil the commission to make disciples. Jesus stirred minds and hearts — and we can grow to do the same. Our teaching actually saves lives.

Whether on a mountainside or on a road, what Jesus said — and how he said it — left a lasting impression. Paul urged us to "pay constant attention to yourself and to your teaching," because doing so leads to the saving of lives. What greater motivation could we have?

1 Timothy 4:16 — "Pay constant attention to yourself and to your teaching… you will save both yourself and those who listen to you."

🔍Look it up: Luke 6:40·Matt. 28:20

Our Grand Instructor & the Master Teacher · the best examples

Heart of it: Jehovah is the greatest Teacher, and Jesus the Master Teacher. Studying how they teach makes us better teachers.

QHow are we taught by Jehovah, the Grand Instructor?

Through his Word, his creation, his spirit, and his organization — "your own eyes will see your Grand Instructor." His holy spirit still functions as a teacher today, bringing things to our minds.

🔍Look it up: Isa. 30:20, 21·John 14:26

QWhy was Jesus such an outstanding teacher — and what was his motive?

He based his teaching on God's Word, not himself; he sought his Father's glory, not his own. He loved both the truths he taught and the people — and mastered teaching methods. We avoid drawing attention to ourselves, which could ensnare us and our student.

🔍Look it up: John 7:16·John 7:18·Matt. 7:28, 29

Preparing to Conduct Studies · the student's needs in mind

Heart of it: Good teaching starts long before the study — with prayerful preparation aimed at reaching the student's heart.

QWhat four things made Ezra an effective teacher?

He prepared his heart to (1) consult God's law, (2) practice it, and (3) teach it. His example: know it, live it, love it, then teach it.

Ezra 7:10 — "Ezra had prepared his heart to consult the law of Jehovah and to practice it, and to teach…"

🔍Look it up: 2 Sam. 12:1-7

QWhat does it mean to prepare with the student's needs in mind?

Ask: "What does the student need? What might he struggle to accept? How can I reach his heart?" Begin with prayer for him, note the lesson title, "read" scriptures, artwork, videos, and summary — and mark key points. It's more than looking up scriptures; it's touching the heart.

🔍Look it up: Acts 16:14·Prov. 15:28·w20.10 p. 8

Help Your Student Prepare · teach them to study

Heart of it: A student who prepares — reading ahead, marking answers, thinking how to say them in his own words — is on the road to real progress.

QHow can showing your studied copy and encouraging your own methods help the student?

"Everyone perfectly instructed will be like his teacher." Show him how you mark answers and look up scriptures, and encourage him to answer in his own words — then prepare a lesson together once.

🔍Look it up: Luke 6:40·w20.10 p. 8, par. 9

Say it your way: What's one habit you'd love to help a student build — and how would you model it?

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: how Jehovah teaches us, Ezra's example, and preparing with the student's heart in mind.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: 1 Timothy 4:16 or Ezra 7:10.
  • Prepare a real lesson from Enjoy Life Forever! with one student's needs in mind.
