Workshop 4 — Conduct Progressive Bible Studies

Practical workshop notes reviewing Units 13 and 14 from "Fully Accomplish Your Ministry" (pt14).

  • Blue links open the source. Top buttons: bigger text, extra spacing, light mode.
Opening Discussion · building on the foundation

Heart of it: Making a disciple is like building on a foundation — Christ Jesus. This workshop helps you find studies, teach better, and lead students to the congregation.

Our goal is more than improving people's lives — it's helping them be "saved through an accurate knowledge of truth." Nothing brings greater joy than seeing your students "go on walking in the truth."

3 John 4 — "Nothing gives me greater joy than this: that I should hear that my children go on walking in the truth."

🔍Look it up: 1 Cor. 3:10-13·1 Tim. 2:4

Focus 1 · Find & Cultivate Studies · plant and water

Heart of it: Studies come from persistent, personal cultivation of every bit of interest.

✓Follow up quickly and warmly.

Return on interest promptly with an appealing subject — and offer a study early, remembering "God kept making it grow."

🔍Look it up: 1 Cor. 3:6

Focus 2 · Improve Your Teaching · reach the heart

Heart of it: Good teaching touches the heart — through questions, illustrations, and simplicity.

✓Use heart-reaching questions.

Ask "Does this seem reasonable to you? How could you apply it?" to find out not just what the student knows, but what he believes.

🔍Look it up: Prov. 20:5

✓Keep it simple.

Focus on the main points; don't overload. Jesus taught profound truths with brevity and clarity.

Focus 3 · Direct Students to the Congregation · the goal is baptism

Heart of it: A study isn't complete until the student is part of the congregation, moving toward baptism.

✓Invite early, build anticipation.

Invite him to meetings from the first study, describe them warmly, and build excitement for the Memorial and assemblies.

Say it your way: After the demonstrations, jot one idea you'll use to help a student make progress toward baptism.

Workshop prep ✅

  • Bring one Bible-study experience — finding, teaching, or bringing a student to meetings.
  • Pick one skill: cultivating interest, heart-reaching teaching, or directing to the congregation.
  • Watch and borrow one good idea from the demonstrations.
