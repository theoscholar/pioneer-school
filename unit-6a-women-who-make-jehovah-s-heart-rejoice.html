Opening Discussion · women who please God

Heart of it: What pleases Jehovah most isn't gender — it's spirituality. The Bible is full of faithful women who made his heart rejoice, and countless do so today.

Fear of God moved two midwives to defy Pharaoh; faith moved Rahab to shield the spies; good sense moved Abigail to prevent bloodshed. Today, in many lands more women than men proclaim the Kingdom message — imitating the faith, discretion, and hospitality of godly women in Scripture.

Psalm 68:11 — "The women proclaiming the good news are a large army."

🔍Look it up: Ex. 1:15-17·Josh. 2:1-7·Rom. 16:12

A Favored Position of Dignity · how God honors women

Heart of it: From the start, God gave the woman an honorable role — and Jesus went out of his way to treat women with respect.

QWhy is the woman's role as "a helper" and "a complement" honorable?

A "complement" completes what's lacking — a vital, valued partner, not an inferior. The same word for "helper" is even used of God himself as our helper.

Genesis 2:18 — "I am going to make a helper for him, as a complement of him."

🔍Look it up: Ps. 33:20·w12 9/1 pp. 4-5

QHow did Jesus show respect for the dignity of women?

He taught women, spoke to them kindly, and even chose women to be the first to witness his resurrection — a striking honor in that culture.

🔍Look it up: Luke 10:38-42·Matt. 28:1, 8-10·w12 9/1 pp. 8-11

QWhat does it mean to assign honor "as to a weaker vessel"?

Like fine, precious china, women are to be treated with special care and honor — a direction that protects and dignifies them, not one that belittles.

🔍Look it up: 1 Pet. 3:7·Prov. 12:4

An Orderly Arrangement · headship that dignifies

Heart of it: Headship isn't about superiority — it's God's loving arrangement for order and peace, and even Jesus gladly submits to his Head.

1 Corinthians 11:3 — "The head of every man is the Christ; in turn, the head of a woman is the man; in turn, the head of the Christ is God."

QHow did Jesus feel about submitting to headship, and why isn't headship demeaning to women?

Jesus willingly submits to his Father — and he's in no way inferior. Likewise, submission doesn't make a woman less valuable; "there is neither male nor female" in standing before God. Order isn't a ranking of worth.

🔍Look it up: Phil. 2:5-8·Gal. 3:26-28

QWhat good can result when a Christian wife respects an unbelieving husband?

Her respectful conduct can win him "without a word" — one formerly harsh husband said his wife's loving respect first aroused his interest in the Bible.

🔍Look it up: 1 Pet. 3:1, 2·Col. 3:18, 23

QWhen would a sister wear a head covering? (the simple principle)

The rule of thumb: a covering is appropriate when she handles something a baptized male would normally do — e.g., a baptized brother is present, she teaches a baptized male, or it's a congregation arrangement. It's not needed in ordinary field ministry with a brother, or when she isn't presiding. (See the chart for each case.)

🔍Look it up: 1 Cor. 11:2-16·w21.02 p. 16, box

Privileges & Responsibilities · a wide field of service

Heart of it: Christian women have rich, meaningful work — teaching in the home, mentoring others, and sharing fully in the lifesaving ministry.

QWhat constructive work can a discerning woman do?

Teach and encourage — raising children in the truth, and (per Titus 2) helping younger women to love their families and God. Older sisters are a treasure of experience.

🔍Look it up: Titus 2:3-5·Prov. 31:1

QIn what vast field have women shared effectively, and why is it vital?

The preaching and teaching work — "your sons and your daughters will prophesy." It's lifesaving: "everyone who calls on the name of Jehovah will be saved," but they need someone to preach to them.

🔍Look it up: Acts 2:17, 18·Rom. 10:13, 14
