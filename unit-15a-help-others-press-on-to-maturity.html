Opening Discussion · milestones, not the finish line

Heart of it: Baptism is a wonderful milestone — but it's the beginning of a lifetime of service. Our job is to help students keep "pressing on to maturity."

Like a birth or a graduation, a student becoming a publisher or getting baptized brings great joy — yet there's still much to do. They've just begun the course, and they still need our training and support.

Hebrews 6:1 — "Now that we have moved beyond the primary doctrine about the Christ, let us press on to maturity."

🔍Look it up: 1 Cor. 3:10-13

Trained to Give a Witness · from student to publisher

Heart of it: From early on, encourage your student to share what he's learning — then train him, step by step, to become a publisher.

QHow do you help a student start speaking to others about the truth?

Encourage it right from the start — some give students tracts to share with friends and relatives, or practice informal witnessing together. Speaking about the truth naturally strengthens his own faith.

🔍Look it up: lff lesson 22·2 Cor. 4:13

QHow do you train a new publisher in witnessing skills?

Three steps, like Gideon's "watch me and do the same": demonstrate how a feature of the ministry is done, let him try it, then give heartfelt commendation. Train him in different forms of witnessing, and stress regular participation.

🔍Look it up: Judg. 7:17·2 Pet. 1:5-8

Toward Dedication & Baptism · track the steps

Heart of it: Baptism is the goal. Watching the natural steps of progress helps you see where a student may need extra help.

QWhat steps show a student progressing toward baptism — and what if he stalls?

He comes to know Jehovah, grows in faith, prays regularly, associates with the congregation, repents, turns around from bad practices, speaks to others, then dedicates himself and gets baptized. If he's stuck at a step, focus loving help right there.

🔍Look it up: John 17:3·Acts 3:19·1 Pet. 3:21

QBefore dedicating, how should a student feel — and why understand what dedication means?

He should love Jehovah and his people deeply — "to do your will… is my delight." Understanding that dedication is a solemn, personal promise to God helps him examine his true motives for baptism.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 40:8·Matt. 24:13

Say it your way: How would you simply explain what "dedication" means to a student? Draft a sentence or two.
Keep Pressing On · beyond baptism

Heart of it: Our care doesn't end at baptism. We keep helping newly baptized ones build a solid foundation for lifelong service.

QHow does our obligation extend beyond baptism?

We keep strengthening and encouraging new ones, as Paul did the disciples. Continue the study until the Enjoy Life Forever! book is finished — even after baptism — and help them build good habits of prayer, study, meeting attendance, and ministry.

🔍Look it up: Acts 14:22·Col. 2:6, 7·1 Pet. 5:8, 9

Say it your way: What's one habit you'd most want a newly baptized friend to have firmly in place — and why?

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: training a new publisher, tracking progress toward baptism, and helping after baptism.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Hebrews 6:1 or Acts 14:22.
  • An experience: have one ready about helping someone progress in the truth.
