Workshop 2 — Various Forms of Our Ministry

Opening Discussion · every opportunity to praise

Heart of it: Workshop 1 focused on house-to-house. This one builds skill in the other forms of ministry — informal and public witnessing — so we grab every chance to praise Jehovah.

Like the first-century Christians who "took advantage of every opportunity," this session helps you develop effectiveness in reaching people wherever they are. Participate fully and come ready to try new ideas.

Hebrews 13:15 — "Let us always offer to God a sacrifice of praise, that is, the fruit of our lips."

🔍Look it up: Acts 20:20, 21

Focus 1 · Build Confidence for Informal Witnessing · just start talking

Heart of it: Confidence grows when you drop the pressure to "give a witness" right away and simply aim to start a friendly conversation.

✓Start with common ground.

A comment on the weather, a purchase, or a news item can break the ice — the good news can come in later, once there's warmth.

🔍Look it up: John 4:7-26·Col. 4:6

✓Be ready with tools.

Carry a tract or jw.org contact card, and keep literature visible when you travel — small things that open doors.

Focus 2 · Speak Up on Appropriate Occasions · prepare your heart

Heart of it: Public witnessing takes a prepared heart. Prayer plus a little courage turns everyday errands into preaching opportunities.

✓Pray, then act.

Ask Jehovah for boldness and the right words — then work in harmony with that prayer. Even a shy child managed it with "Ready, set, go!"

🔍Look it up: Eph. 6:19·1 Thess. 2:2

✓Read the person.

Notice when someone is interested — or when it's time to leave a good impression and stop. Both are skills worth practicing.

Focus 3 · Find the Joy · more ways, more joy

Heart of it: Sharing in various forms of the ministry stretches us — and the growth brings real joy.

✓Try something new.

Pick one method you haven't used much — a cart shift, public transport, or informal witnessing to a neighbor — and give it a go.

Say it your way: After the workshop, jot one suggestion you found helpful and plan to try this week.

Workshop prep ✅

  • Bring one informal-witnessing experience — yours or one you've read.
  • Pick one skill to focus on: starting conversations, praying for boldness, or reading the person.
  • Watch and borrow one good idea from the demonstrations.
