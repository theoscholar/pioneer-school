Opening Discussion · the greatest issue

Heart of it: The biggest question in the universe is whether Jehovah's name will be made holy and his right to rule upheld. You get to answer "yes" every day — and help others do the same.

Most of the world chases self-rule, and it has bred confusion and moral breakdown. You've chosen the opposite: to love and uphold Jehovah's sovereignty. As a pioneer, your example teaches people that God's rule isn't a burden — it's worthy of full support and brings rich rewards.

Revelation 4:11 — "You are worthy, Jehovah our God, to receive the glory and the honor and the power, because you created all things."

🔍Look it up: Ezek. 36:23·Ps. 119:1·it-2 pp. 1008-1009

All Mankind Depends on Jehovah · his rightful place

Heart of it: Jehovah is the rightful Ruler of everything because he is the Creator and Life-Giver — and we simply can't succeed by directing our own steps.

QWhat is "sovereignty," and why is Jehovah the rightful Universal Sovereign?

Sovereignty is supreme rulership. Jehovah holds it by right because he created all things and gives everyone life — "to whom do I owe anything?" No one can out-rank the Maker.

🔍Look it up: Job 41:11·Ps. 24:1·it-2 p. 1008

QTo what extent do all people depend on Jehovah — and why can't we direct our own step?

Completely: "by him we have life and move and exist." Jeremiah honestly admitted a man's way is not his own to direct. We show we accept God's rule by letting his Word guide us.

Jeremiah 10:23 — "I well know, O Jehovah, that man's way does not belong to him. It does not belong to man who is walking even to direct his step."

🔍Look it up: Acts 17:25, 28·Ps. 143:10·Isa. 54:13

Subjection Requires Loyalty — From the Heart · when no one's watching

Heart of it: Upholding Jehovah's rule isn't just outward obedience — it's loyalty that holds firm under pressure and stays true even in private.

QWhat is loyalty, and how has Jehovah shown it?

Loyalty is faithful, loving attachment that won't let go. Jehovah models it perfectly — he is "loyal" and merciful, never abandoning his devoted ones.

🔍Look it up: Jer. 3:12·Rev. 15:4·it-2 pp. 280-281

QHow do we stay loyal when others speak against Jehovah, or amid racism and nationalism?

We don't join in or stay silently agreeable — we stand with God and his people. The truth that "God is not partial" frees us from every prejudice.

🔍Look it up: Acts 10:34, 35·Rev. 2:10

QHow do we show heart-deep subjection in private — reading, entertainment, devices, home life?

By choosing what pleases Jehovah even when no one would know. Godly subjection never depends on being seen or on fear of being caught — it flows from love for his ways.

🔍Look it up: 1 Pet. 1:22·Prov. 4:4, 10-13

Say it your way: Name one private area where upholding Jehovah's rule matters most to you — and why it's worth it.
Expressed Through the Congregation — Forever · the settled question

Heart of it: We show submission to Jehovah by supporting the arrangements he uses — and one day his rulership will be vindicated for all time.

QHow can you demonstrate submission through the congregation — and how do you benefit?

By sharing in the preaching work, respecting the "faithful and discreet slave," keeping the congregation clean, and supporting the elders. Submitting brings unity, protection, and peace.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 28:19, 20·Matt. 24:45-47·Heb. 10:23-25

QWhat final test comes after the Thousand Year Reign, and why will the issue never need settling again?

Satan is released for a final test of mankind, then destroyed. After Jesus hands the Kingdom back to his Father, sovereignty is vindicated once and for all — the question is settled forever.

🔍Look it up: Rev. 20:7-9·1 Cor. 15:24-28

Say it your way: How does knowing this issue will be settled forever affect how you view today's hardships?

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Revelation 4:11 or Jeremiah 10:23.
  • 1 self-check: "Am I living my whole life in full submission to Jehovah's sovereignty?"
