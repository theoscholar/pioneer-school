Opening Discussion · light that keeps growing

Heart of it: Jehovah reveals truth a little at a time, like a sunrise that keeps getting brighter. Our job is to keep walking forward in that growing light.

Even Jesus' own disciples still had much to learn — deep questions (like who could share in the new covenant) needed holy spirit to unlock. Jesus promised such truths would be revealed progressively. Jehovah still sheds light today, refining us in teaching, conduct, and organization.

Proverbs 4:18 — "The path of the righteous is like the bright morning light that grows brighter and brighter until full day."

QHow is Jehovah the Source of all spiritual enlightenment, and why appreciate that he reveals things progressively?

All light comes from him — "in your light we see light." He reveals truth step by step so we're never overwhelmed, and each new flash deepens our appreciation.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 43:3·Isa. 42:6, 7·Prov. 4:18·John 16:12

QWhat was Jesus' role in illuminating Jehovah's purposes?

Jesus is "the light of the world" — he reflected and taught his Father's truth, and through him "the light of the knowledge of God's glory" shines on us.

🔍Look it up: John 9:5·John 12:35·2 Cor. 4:6

True Worshippers in the First Century · the pattern

Heart of it: When new light came, first-century Christians didn't resist — they trusted the arrangement Jehovah was using and moved ahead together.

QHow did responsible brothers respond to progressive enlightenment, and on what basis did the governing body decide?

Faced with the circumcision issue, they gathered, weighed the Scriptures and the evidence of holy spirit, and reached a united decision — "the holy spirit and we ourselves have favored."

🔍Look it up: Acts 15:7-12

QHow were the decisions shared, and how did Christians show willingness to walk by spirit?

The decrees were delivered to the congregations, and the brothers gladly accepted them — so the congregations "continued to be made firm... and to increase in number."

🔍Look it up: Acts 16:4, 5·Acts 15:25-31

True Worshippers Today · refined and blessed

Heart of it: Jehovah uses "the faithful and discreet slave" to feed us today. Refinements in teaching, morals, and organization are proof of his loving guidance.

QWhom did Jesus appoint to feed his sheep, and why does studying prophecy build faith?

Jesus appointed "the faithful and discreet slave" to give spiritual food at the proper time. Studying fulfilled prophecy shows Jehovah keeps his word — even angels longed to peer into these things.

🔍Look it up: w13 7/15 p. 22, par. 10·1 Pet. 1:10-12

QHow have doctrinal and moral refinements benefited us?

Clearer understanding of God's spiritual temple and of texts like Ezekiel 38, the fruitage of the spirit, and the model prayer has sharpened our faith. Refinements in morality help God's people glorify his name "in word and deed."

🔍Look it up: w10 7/15 p. 22·Ezek. 38:2, 4·Gal. 5:22, 23·kr pp. 110-113

QWhat organizational developments fulfil Isaiah 60:17, and how does jw.org show the slave's faith and discretion?

Progressive improvements in oversight and arrangements fulfil the promise of "peace" as overseers and "righteousness" as taskmasters. And jw.org puts an ocean of accurate spiritual food in hundreds of languages at everyone's fingertips.

🔍Look it up: Isa. 60:17·w14 1/15 p. 15, par. 12

Personally Keeping Pace · your part

Heart of it: The right attitude is the key. When an adjustment comes, humble hearts move ahead with the organization — they don't get left behind.

QWhat do we learn from Israel and "the pillar of fire and cloud"?

When it moved, Israel moved — promptly and trustingly. We keep our eyes on Jehovah's guidance and follow without delay.

🔍Look it up: Ex. 14:24·w11 4/15 pp. 3-5

QWhat attitude helps us keep walking in increasing light, even when a cherished custom is adjusted?

Humility and trust. One longtime elder said the key is "having the right attitude" — refusing a refinement means being left behind. Like Peter, we ask, "Where else would we go?"

John 6:68 — "Lord, whom shall we go away to? You have sayings of everlasting life."

🔍Look it up: w11 9/15 p. 14, pars. 14-15·w21.05 pp. 5-6

QHow do we let our light shine — and why keep walking in it?

By sharing the truth we've received far and wide — "let your light shine before men." We keep walking because staying current with Jehovah's guidance keeps us close to him and useful in the ministry.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 5:14-16·Ps. 97:11·2 Pet. 1:19-21

Say it your way: Name one recent "flash of light" (a refinement or new understanding) you've personally appreciated, and why.

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: the first-century pattern, a refinement you appreciate, and the right attitude toward change.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Proverbs 4:18 or John 6:68.
  • Theme: Luke 12:12 — "the holy spirit will teach you."
