Opening Discussion · what endurance really is

Heart of it: Endurance isn't grim resignation — it's the spirit that "can bear things… with blazing hope," keeping us on our feet with our face to the wind because it sees the goal beyond the pain.

We need endurance not only under persecution but in everyday life — health problems, family opposition, a slow territory, or a battle with our own shortcomings. The great question: can we keep our joy while enduring? Yes, we can.

Hebrews 10:36 — "You need endurance, so that after you have done the will of God, you may receive the fulfillment of the promise."

🔍Look it up: 2 Pet. 1:5, 6

Examples of Endurance · learn from the faithful

Heart of it: Jehovah filled the Bible with examples of endurance — above all Jesus — so we could draw comfort and strength from them.

QWhy did God include examples of endurance, and what do we learn from Jesus and Job?

"For all the things that were written beforehand were written for our instruction" (Rom. 15:4) — so we'd have comfort and hope. Jesus endured "for the joy that was set before him" (Heb. 12:2). Job saw "the outcome Jehovah gave" (James 5:11) and came to know God better through trial: "now I do see you" (Job 42:5).

🔍Look it up: Rom. 15:4·Heb. 12:2, 3·Jas. 5:10, 11

"You Need Endurance" · in real situations

Heart of it: Endurance shows in everyday things — an early start, hard weather, a personality clash, an indifferent territory — and in bigger trials too. Practical helps make it possible.

QWhy not be quick to evade situations that require endurance?

Because "tribulation produces endurance" (Rom. 5:3), and enduring reveals a fine heart that will "bear fruit with endurance" (Luke 8:15). We needn't dread future trials — Jehovah "will… make the way out" (1 Cor. 10:13) so we can endure.

🔍Look it up: Rom. 5:3, 4·Luke 8:15·1 Cor. 10:13

QWhat helps us endure trials like family opposition, poor health, or pressure at work?

Jehovah's provisions: personal study and meditation, Christian association, the ministry itself, prayer, and a good conscience. One brother endured months of ridicule Christlike-ly — and 24 years later learned the man had become a baptized Witness, never forgetting that witness who endured.

🔍Look it up: Ps. 55:22·Matt. 11:28-30·Heb. 10:24, 25

Say it your way: Which provision (study, association, ministry, prayer) has helped you endure most? Share why.
Let Endurance Complete Its Work — The Reward · the goal in view

Heart of it: Endurance polishes fine qualities in us and leads to a grand reward. And this very school has fortified you to keep going.

QHow does endurance "complete" us?

Trial after trial exercises godly qualities and awakens new ones, so we become "complete… lacking in nothing." That's how we grow "perfect" — whole and fully rounded — in God's service.

James 1:2-4 — "Consider it all joy… knowing as you do that this tested quality of your faith produces endurance. But let endurance complete its work."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 5:48

QHow has this school fortified you — and what is the reward for endurance?

Reflect on what you've learned that will help you stay in pioneer service and encourage others. Love makes us endure all things (1 Cor. 13:7). The reward is priceless: "by your endurance you will preserve your lives" (Luke 21:19) — "the crown of life."

James 1:12 — "Happy is the man who keeps on enduring trial, because on becoming approved he will receive the crown of life."

🔍Look it up: Luke 21:19·1 Cor. 13:7, 8

Say it your way: What have you learned in this school that will help you keep enduring in the pioneer service?
