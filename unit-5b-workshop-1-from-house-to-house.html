Workshop 1 — From House to House

Practical workshop notes reviewing Units 4(b) and 5(a) from "Fully Accomplish Your Ministry" (pt14).

How to use these notes 💡
  • This is a workshop — less about new facts, more about doing. It reviews Units 4(b) and 5(a) and puts them into practice.
  • Watch the demonstrations, join in, and note your own strengths and things to work on.
  • Blue links open the source. Top buttons: bigger text, extra spacing, light mode.
Opening Discussion · put it into practice

Heart of it: Jesus gave his followers clear, practical instruction for the ministry — and it blessed them. This workshop turns the last two lessons into real skills.

The early Christians preached "every day… from house to house" without letup, and Paul did the same. In this workshop the instructor reviews key lessons and focuses on practical application. Participate fully — and come ready to share introductions that work well in your territory.

Acts 5:42 — "Every day in the temple and from house to house they continued without letup teaching and declaring the good news."

🔍Look it up: Luke 10:1-12·Acts 20:20·w12 3/15 p. 12

Focus 1 · Prepare for the Ministry · ready before you knock

Heart of it: A little preparation turns nerves into confidence. Know your introduction, your publication, and your first question.

✓Have an introduction ready that fits your territory.

Prepare a warm opening and a thought-provoking question — be ready to share one that works well for you.

✓Think about the person before you speak.

Recall Unit 4(b): notice surroundings and current events; prepare with the householder's needs in mind.

🔍Look it up: Phil. 2:4

Focus 2 · Show Interest & Start Conversations · make a connection

Heart of it: The goal isn't to deliver a speech — it's to start a friendly, two-way conversation.

✓Lead with genuine interest.

A warm smile, a sincere question, and real listening open more doors than any script. Let the person talk.

🔍Look it up: Jas. 1:19·Col. 4:6

✓Adapt to who answers the door.

Adjust your manner for the young, the elderly, the busy, or the grieving — "all things to people of all sorts."

🔍Look it up: 1 Cor. 9:22

Focus 3 · Keep a Positive Attitude · joy that lasts

Heart of it: A cheerful, confident spirit is contagious — and it comes from remembering why we do this work.

✓See people as Jesus did.

Compassion, not frustration — even a poor response doesn't dim our joy, because we're imitating our Master.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 9:36·Matt. 10:22

✓Learn from the workshop itself.

Watch the teaching methods, welcome new ideas, and notice your own strengths to build on. That's how confidence grows.

Say it your way: After the demonstrations, jot one idea you'll try in your own ministry this week.

Workshop prep ✅

  • Bring one introduction that works well in your territory — be ready to share it.
  • Pick one skill to focus on: preparing, showing interest, or staying positive.
  • Watch and borrow: note one good technique from a fellow student or instructor.
