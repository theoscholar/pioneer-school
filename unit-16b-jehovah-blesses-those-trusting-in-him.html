Jehovah Blesses Those Trusting In Him

Opening Discussion · the most trustworthy Person

Heart of it: No one is more trustworthy than Jehovah. To "trust in Jehovah" means letting his thinking guide ours — in all our ways, not just in a crisis.

It's easier to read Proverbs 3:5, 6 than to live it. Trusting Jehovah means recognizing that his wisdom is infinitely superior to our own, and leaning on it instead of on our own understanding. He blesses those who put their complete trust in him.

Proverbs 3:5, 6 — "Trust in Jehovah with all your heart, and do not rely on your own understanding. In all your ways take notice of him."

🔍Look it up: Isa. 55:8, 9·Rom. 12:1

Jehovah — and Those Who Trusted Him · real examples

Heart of it: Jehovah's loyal love makes him utterly reliable — and Jesus and a humble widow show what trusting him looks like.

QHow is Jehovah's trustworthiness anchored in his "loyal love"?

His loyal love is steadfast and enduring — he is "merciful and compassionate… abundant in loyal love and truth" (Ex. 34:6). He becomes whatever is needed to bless and protect his people.

🔍Look it up: Ex. 34:6, 7·Zeph. 3:12, 13

QWhat do Jesus and the widow of Zarephath teach us about trust?

Jesus answered every temptation with "It is written" — his knowledge of Scripture anchored his trust. The widow put Jehovah first, even when down to her last meal — and he sustained her flour and oil. Trust means acting on God's word before we see the outcome.

🔍Look it up: 1 John 2:15, 16·1 Ki. 17:13-16·Matt. 4:4

Trust Jehovah's Promises · not one has failed

Heart of it: Jehovah's track record is perfect. Meditating on his past acts of deliverance fortifies us for whatever lies ahead.

Joshua 23:14 — "Not one word out of all the good promises… has failed. They have all come true for you."

QHow does recalling past deliverances strengthen our faith?

Noah, the Red Sea, Hezekiah's day — time and again Jehovah rescued his people. Remembering that he kept every promise gives us confidence he'll deliver us too. Like the prophet, we "exult in Jehovah" and are "joyful in the God of my salvation" (Hab. 3:18).

🔍Look it up: Hab. 3:18·Gen. 7:1-5·2 Ki. 19:32-35

Say it your way: Recall one time you trusted Jehovah and he came through. That experience is a perfect comment.
Blessings Overtake Those Who Trust · his almighty power

Heart of it: Jehovah uses his almighty power to protect those who trust him — and his blessings truly "overtake" them (Deut. 28:2).

QWhat can we be sure of about Jehovah's promises and protection?

We can have complete faith in everything he says — he is "the personification of truth." Those who make him their "refuge" and "stronghold" (Ps. 91:2) are shielded, and his word never returns without accomplishing its purpose.

🔍Look it up: Deut. 28:1, 2·Ps. 91:1, 2·Isa. 55:8-11

Say it your way: In what area of life do you most want to grow in trusting Jehovah? One honest sentence.

  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Proverbs 3:5, 6 or Joshua 23:14.
  • An experience: have one ready about trusting Jehovah through a hard time.
