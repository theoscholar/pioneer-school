Opening Discussion · the Master worth imitating

Heart of it: We love Jesus even though we've never seen him — because he perfectly reflects his Father. Learning from the Master means copying the kind of person he was.

Jesus showed mildness, empathy, and interest in others; he was approachable, hard-working, and wise with material things. He learned all this from Jehovah — and now teaches us. We show we're learning from him by displaying those same qualities and drawing others to our way of life.

1 Peter 1:8 — "Though you never saw him, you love him."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 11:28-30·John 13:13

Love — Jesus' Dominant Quality · the mark of a disciple

Heart of it: Above all, Jesus was defined by love — and he made self-sacrificing love the identifying mark of his true followers.

John 13:34, 35 — "By this all will know that you are my disciples—if you have love among yourselves."

QHow do we know love was Jesus' dominant quality — and what did his love always highlight?

He said, "no one has love greater than this" — and proved it by dying for us. His love always pointed to his Father: he did what pleased God and kept his commandments.

🔍Look it up: John 15:13, 14·John 8:29·1 John 5:3

QHow can you show Jesus-like love — toward the brothers and toward the world?

Toward fellow believers: compassion, patience, forgiveness ("clothe yourselves with love"). Toward the world: gracious, salt-seasoned speech that draws people to the truth. "The love the Christ has compels us."

🔍Look it up: Col. 3:12-17·Col. 4:5, 6·2 Cor. 5:14, 15

Balance & Following His Steps · work, rest, and endurance

Heart of it: Jesus kept material things in their place, valued rest and wholesome association, and left us a model for handling hard times.

QWhat was Jesus' attitude toward material things, and toward work and recreation?

"Keep on… seeking first the Kingdom" — he trusted his Father to provide. He worked hard, but also enjoyed meals and gatherings and urged his apostles to "rest up a little." Balance, not extremes.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 6:24-33·Luke 12:15·Mark 6:31

QHow is Jesus a model in challenging times?

Under insult and suffering he "did not threaten" but entrusted himself to God. Following his steps closely, we handle opposition with calm faith — resolved to keep learning from him.

1 Peter 2:21 — "Christ suffered for you, leaving you a model for you to follow his steps closely."

🔍Look it up: 1 Pet. 2:21-23·John 14:21, 23

Say it your way: Which quality of Jesus do you most want to imitate right now — and why?

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: why we love Jesus, love as the mark of a disciple, and following his steps in hard times.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: 1 Peter 1:8 or John 13:34, 35.
  • Reflect: which of Jesus' qualities draws people to you most?
