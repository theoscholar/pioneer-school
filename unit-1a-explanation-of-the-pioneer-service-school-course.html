Opening Discussion · why this school exists

Heart of it: Jehovah is the greatest Teacher in the universe, and this school is one more gift from him to help you shine in the ministry.

The Bible calls Jehovah the "Grand Instructor." He started teaching the moment he created his firstborn Son, and he never stopped — even after Adam sinned he kept lovingly educating people. In our day, "the faithful and discreet slave" (Matt. 24:45) provides a feast of spiritual food, including schools like this one. So the Pioneer Service School isn't a test to survive; it's a loving provision to enjoy.

Isaiah 30:20 — Jehovah is "your Grand Instructor," and your eyes will see him.

🔍Look it up: Isa. 30:20·John 8:28·Isa. 48:17, 18·Matt. 24:45-47

What this course will do for you · the goals

Heart of it: Four take-aways — a closer bond with God, more love for the brothers, sharper ministry skills, and better use of Bible principles.

1Strengthen your relationship with Jehovah and with Jesus.

Everything else grows out of this. Knowing God and his Son is the very definition of everlasting life.

John 17:3 — "This means everlasting life, their coming to know you, the only true God, and the one whom you sent, Jesus Christ."

2Abound in love for the whole brotherhood.

The week is shoulder-to-shoulder with fellow pioneers — a chance to widen your love for the family of believers.

🔍Look it up: 1 Pet. 2:17

3Become more effective in every part of your ministry.

40 hours of discussion, demonstrations, and workshops to sharpen your skills so you're "completely equipped" (2 Tim. 3:17).

🔍Look it up: 2 Tim. 3:17

4Be more aware of Bible principles — and better at applying them.

Not just facts, but wisdom you can put to work in real life and real ministry.

🔍Look it up: Col. 1:9, 10

How to get the most out of it · your part

Heart of it: What you get out of the school depends on what you put in. Prepare, join in, and keep asking "how does this apply to me?"

✓Prepare before class.

Study and meditate on the lessons, questions, and scriptures ahead of time. Get good rest so you're alert. The more attention you give, the more you'll gain.

🔍Look it up: Luke 6:38·Mark 4:24

✓Join in the discussions.

Your comments and experiences help the others, just as theirs help you — a real "interchange of encouragement" (Rom. 1:12).

🔍Look it up: Rom. 1:11, 12

✓Don't be nervous — and don't compare.

There are no written tests. You benefit by doing your best and not measuring yourself against anyone else.

🔍Look it up: Gal. 6:4

✓Make it personal.

Throughout the week, keep asking yourself: How does this apply to me? What principle is involved, and how can I use it? How will it enrich my friendship with Jehovah? A wise person keeps growing.

🔍Look it up: Prov. 9:9

Say it your way: Write one goal for the week — something you personally want to walk away better at.

The whole point, in one verse ✅

2 Timothy 4:5 — "You, though, keep your senses in all things, endure hardship, do the work of an evangelizer, fully accomplish your ministry."
  • Come prepared, come rested, come ready to comment.
  • Treat every lesson as a gift from your Grand Instructor.
  • Keep one question in your back pocket all week: "How can I apply this?"
