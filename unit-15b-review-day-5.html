Review: Unit 13(a) · Learn From the Master
John 14:23 — "If anyone loves me, he will observe my word."

●Why can we rightly call Jesus our Master, and how can you imitate him?

He perfectly reflects Jehovah — so we copy his love, empathy, industriousness, and balance with material things.

Review: Unit 13(b) · Progressive Bible Studies, Part 1
Matthew 28:20 — "…teaching them to observe all the things I have commanded you."

●How do you prepare a study with the student's needs in mind?

Pray about him, then ask "What does he need? What might he struggle with? How can I reach his heart?" — and prepare accordingly.

Review: Unit 14(a) · Progressive Bible Studies, Part 2
Luke 6:40 — "Everyone who is perfectly instructed will be like his teacher."

●How can you imitate Jesus' use of questions and illustrations?

Questions draw out the heart; simple illustrations make truth stick. Keep profound truths clear and simple.

Review: Unit 14(b) & 15(a) · Workshop 4 / Press On to Maturity
3 John 4 — "I should hear that my children go on walking in the truth."

●How do you help a student make progress toward baptism?

Track the steps of progress, train him to witness, explain dedication, and keep helping even after baptism.
