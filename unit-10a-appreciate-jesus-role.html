Opening Discussion · why this matters

Heart of it: Knowing Jesus' role isn't just head knowledge. It's the door to our friendship with Jehovah — and it fuels how we help others in the ministry.

Jesus told his apostles they would "be witnesses" of him "to the most distant part of the earth." When you talk to people about God's promises, you are doing exactly that. So why care so much about God's firstborn Son? Because Jesus said no one comes to the Father except through him. Understanding his role grows our appreciation for God's whole purpose — and moves us to help sincere people of every background get to know him.

Acts 1:8 — "But you will receive power when the holy spirit comes upon you, and you will be witnesses of me in Jerusalem, in all Judea and Samaria, and to the most distant part of the earth."
John 14:6 — "I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me."

The four sections below unpack that one verse: Jesus as The Person, then The Way, The Truth, and The Life.

The Person · who Jesus is

Heart of it: Jesus is God's very first creation — his firstborn, only-begotten Son — used by Jehovah to make everything else. Great, but not equal to God.

QHow do we know Jesus is God's "firstborn" Son?

The Bible calls him "the firstborn of all creation," meaning he was Jehovah's first and direct creation — created before everything else. Jesus himself pointed to God as the source of his life: "I live because of the Father" (John 6:57). So Jehovah was truly his Father and Life-Giver.

Colossians 1:15 — "He is the image of the invisible God, the firstborn of all creation."

🔎Look it up: Col. 1:15

QWhy is Jesus also called God's "only-begotten" Son?

Because he is the only one Jehovah created directly, by himself. Every other thing — angels, stars, humans — was made through Jesus. That direct, one-of-a-kind origin makes him "only-begotten."

🔎Look it up: John 1:3, 14·Heb. 11:17·it-2 p. 53, par. 2

QWhat was Jesus' role in creation (Colossians 1:16)? Why is this not "co-Creator"?

Jesus was the skilled worker Jehovah used to create all other things — the agent, not the source. A helper who builds a house from the owner's plans is not a "co-owner." Insight puts it simply: God's wisdom was expressed in creation, but through his Son. Jehovah is the Creator; Jesus is the one he worked through.

Colossians 1:16 — "By means of him all other things were created in the heavens and on the earth, the things visible and the things invisible."

🔎Look it up: Matt. 19:4-6·it-2 p. 52

QIn what sense is Jesus "wisdom personified" (Proverbs 8)?

Proverbs pictures wisdom as a person working "beside" Jehovah as a "master worker." That fits God's firstborn Son. Wisdom only shows up when it's put to work — and Jehovah's wisdom was put to work in creation through Jesus.

🔎Look it up: Prov. 8:12, 30·1 Cor. 1:24·it-2 p. 52

QIn what way is Jesus "the Word" (John 1:1)?

"The Word" is a job title: he is God's Spokesman — the one who speaks and acts for Jehovah. He still carries the name "The Word of God" today in heaven. We can imitate his humility by happily filling the role Jehovah gives us, without needing the spotlight.

John 1:1 — "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was a god."

🔎Look it up: John 1:1, 14·John 12:50·it-2 p. 53, pars. 3-5

QHow would you help someone understand Jesus' prehuman existence — and why does it matter to you?

Jesus lived as a mighty spirit person for ages before coming to earth (John's Gospel and Micah 5:2 point to this). Personally, it deepens my awe: Jehovah handed his dearest, most experienced Son to rescue us. That's how much we're loved.

🔎Look it up: John 8:23, 42, 58·Micah 5:2

"The Way" · our road back to God

Heart of it: Sinful humans can't reach Jehovah on their own. Jesus is the one and only bridge back — for friendship, forgiveness, and prayer.

QWhat is the only way for sinful humans to be reconciled to God?

Through Jesus' ransom. Insight says it plainly: "Jesus Christ is the one and only 'way' of reconciliation with God." His sacrifice repairs the gap that sin opened up.

🔎Look it up: Rom. 5:11·it-2 pp. 760-762

QHow does Jesus make a good standing with Jehovah possible?

He is the mediator — the go-between. He gave his perfect life as "a corresponding ransom," so imperfect people can be counted righteous and approach God.

1 Timothy 2:5, 6 — "For there is one God, and one mediator between God and men, a man, Christ Jesus, who gave himself a corresponding ransom for all."

🔎Look it up: Rom. 5:18·1 Tim. 2:5·Heb. 9:22-26

QWho can benefit — and what's the final outcome?

People of every nation. When Christ died, the wall between Jew and Gentile came down. Peter learned "God is not partial." Those who accept this provision can be "completely saved," with everlasting life ahead.

🔎Look it up: Acts 10:43·Eph. 2:18·1 John 2:2·Heb. 7:25

QHow is Jesus "the way" in prayer, and in the example he set?

We pray to Jehovah, through Jesus — Jesus opened that channel. And his flawless life gives us a model to "follow his steps closely." Accepting his role means both praying in his name and copying his example.

🔎Look it up: John 16:23, 24·1 Pet. 2:21

"The Truth" · God's promises made real

Heart of it: Jesus didn't just teach truth — his whole life turned God's promises into reality. He is the living proof that Jehovah keeps his word.

QHow did Jesus "bear witness to the truth" by his entire life course?

Everything he did — his birth, baptism, three-and-a-half-year ministry, faithful death, and resurrection — proved God's promises true. Insight says "the truth came to be through Jesus": God's truth "came to realization" in him. He was the real Messiah, not a shadow.

John 1:17 — "The undeserved kindness and the truth came to be through Jesus Christ."

🔎Look it up: John 18:37·Luke 24:44·John 1:17·it-2 p. 60, par. 2·p. 62, pars. 1-5·p. 692, par. 2

QHow is Jesus a key part of these prophecies?

He is the thread that ties them together:

Genesis 3:15 — He is the promised "seed" who crushes the serpent (Satan).
Genesis 22:16-18 — He is the offspring of Abraham through whom all nations are blessed.
Daniel 7:13, 14 — He is the "son of man" given rulership, honor, and an everlasting Kingdom.

As Revelation 19:10 says, "the witness concerning Jesus is what inspires prophecy" — he's the point they all aim at.

🔎Look it up: Rev. 19:10·it-2 p. 60

QWhat shows Jesus never held back from speaking the truth — and how can you imitate his zeal?

He preached "from city to city and village to village," never softening the message to please people. I imitate his zeal by speaking up kindly but clearly, even when it's easier to stay quiet.

🔎Look it up: Luke 8:1

QHow does the witness concerning Jesus inspire prophecy?

Jesus is the heart of Bible prophecy — "the witness concerning Jesus is what inspires prophecy." So the whole "work of witnessing concerning Jesus" is a sacred, prophetic privilege, not a small thing.

🔎Look it up: Rev. 19:10·it-2 p. 691, par. 4

"The Life" · the gift of living forever

Heart of it: Jesus came to give life back to the human family. His ransom is the price that buys us out of sin and death.

QFor what reason did Jesus come to earth?

Not to be served, but to give his life as a ransom for many. That was the mission.

Matthew 20:28 — "…just as the Son of man came, not to be ministered to, but to minister and to give his life as a ransom in exchange for many."

🔎Look it up: Matt. 20:28

QWhat is the ransom price Jesus paid?

His own perfect human life — his "precious blood," like that of a spotless lamb. A perfect life was the exact match needed to buy back what Adam lost.

1 Peter 1:18, 19 — "…with precious blood, like that of an unblemished and spotless lamb, that of Christ."

🔎Look it up: 1 Pet. 1:18, 19

QWhat does "Chief Agent of life" mean (Acts 3:15)?

The Greek means "chief leader" or "pioneer of Life." Jesus opens the road to eternal life and administers its blessings — as High Priest who cleanses us from sin and as the appointed Judge. He leads the way to life, then hands out its benefits.

Acts 3:15 — "…the Chief Agent of life. But God raised him up from the dead, of which fact we are witnesses."

🔎Look it up: Acts 3:15·Acts 10:42·John 6:39, 40·Eph. 1:7·Heb. 8:1-3·it-2 p. 61, pars. 1-3

QHow are we already benefiting from Jesus' sacrifice — and if you want everlasting life, in whom must you have faith?

Even now a great crowd enjoys a clean standing and peace with God because of the ransom. And the door to eternal life has one key: faith in Jesus. "There is no other name" by which we can be saved. Understanding him as "the life" makes my ministry feel urgent — I'm offering people the one name that saves.

Acts 4:12 — "There is no salvation in anyone else, for there is no other name under heaven… by which we must get saved."

🔎Look it up: Rev. 7:9, 10·Acts 4:10, 12
