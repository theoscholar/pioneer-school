Footnotes & Marginal References · hidden helpers on the page

Heart of it: The little letters and numbers on each page are treasure — they open up alternate meanings, cross-links, and fulfilled prophecy.

Psalm 119:97 — "How I do love your law! I ponder over it all day long."

QWhat do the four kinds of footnotes do? (A2)

Each adds accuracy:

"Or" — an equally valid rendering (Ps. 51:5, Luke 14:26). "Or possibly" — a reasonable alternative (Eccl. 7:9, Gal. 5:14). "Lit." — the literal wording behind the smooth translation (John 2:4). Meaning / background — explains names or customs (Gen. 25:8, Judg. 7:19).

🔍Look it up: nwt p. 1725

QWhat's the value of "See Glossary" and footnotes that point to the appendixes?

"See Glossary" flags a term explained in the back (like "Gehenna" or "soul"). Appendix footnotes (as at John 12:38) send you to a fuller explanation — deeper understanding in one tap.

🔍Look it up: Matt. 24:3·nwt p. 1723

QHow do the marginal (cross) references help?

They link related verses: parallel accounts, fulfilled prophecy (compare Ps. 22:1 with Matt. 27:46), direct quotes, and Law-covenant patterns fulfilled in the Greek Scriptures. Great for letting the Bible explain itself.

🔍Look it up: Deut. 8:3·Matt. 4:4

Indexes, Glossary & Appendixes · the back of the Bible

Heart of it: The back pages turn your Bible into a research kit — find topics, define terms, and picture the settings.

QHow can you use the "Bible Words Index" and the Glossary?

The Index helps you find verses by topic — e.g., look up "Comfort" to gather encouraging texts for a shepherding visit. The Glossary defines Bible terms in plain language — perfect for family worship or personal study.

🔍Look it up: Index: nwt pp. 1664-1691·Glossary: nwt pp. 1692-1716

QHow do the charts and maps (A6, A7, Appendix B) help?

The Prophets and Kings chart lines up who ruled when. "Main Events of Jesus' Earthly Life" sets the four Gospels in order. Appendix B's maps, diagrams, and tables of time and measurements make Bible accounts come alive.

🔍Look it up: Chart: nwt pp. 1744-1747·A7: nwt pp. 1748-1763·Appx B: nwt p. 1765

"Introduction to God's Word" & the Study Bible · tools for teaching

Heart of it: Two more gifts: a simple 32-page overview to use with people, and study notes to go deeper yourself.

QHow can you use the 32-page "An Introduction to God's Word"?

It answers big Bible questions with pictures and verses — ideal from house to house, on return visits, to start or enrich a study, and as practice material for meetings for field service.

🔍Look it up: nwt pp. 5-36

QHow do the book outlines and Study Bible notes help?

The outline at the start of each Bible book helps you find accounts fast. The Study Bible notes explain verses, customs, and pictures — helping you study more deeply and prepare richer answers.

🔍Look it up: nwt p. 1725
