Review: Unit 4(a) · Sovereignty & Sanctification of Jehovah's Name
Psalm 143:10 — "Teach me to do your will, for you are my God."

●How can pioneers uphold Jehovah's sovereignty?

By submitting to his rule in every area — even in private — and by our example teaching others that his rulership is worthy of full support.

●How can you help others cultivate love for his sovereignty?

By showing that obeying Jehovah isn't a burden but the path to real freedom and blessing.

Review: Unit 4(b) · Show Personal Interest in Others
Philippians 2:4 — "Look out… for the interests of others."

●How will you show genuine interest, and why is listening essential?

Warm attention and real listening — people often remember how well we listened more than what we said.

●What's the most important reason to show personal interest?

To imitate Jehovah's love and draw people to him — not as a technique, but from the heart.

Review: Unit 5(a) · From House to House
Matthew 28:19 — "Make disciples of people of all the nations."

●How will meditating on Jesus' example increase your zeal?

Recalling that preaching was his life's work — and how he rose early to reach more people — reignites our own drive.

●How can you motivate others to be zealous house to house?

By sharing your joy and the good results, and by helping them see people with Jesus' compassion.

Review: Unit 5(b) · Workshop 1
Acts 5:42 — "Every day… from house to house they continued… teaching and declaring the good news."

●What practical things will you do to start conversations, and stay adaptable?

Prepare a warm introduction and question; then adjust to whoever answers — young, elderly, busy, or grieving.

●How can you help others find joy in the ministry?

By your positive spirit — joy is contagious, and it comes from remembering why we do the work.

Review: Unit 6(a) · Women Who Make Jehovah's Heart Rejoice
Psalm 68:11 — "The women proclaiming the good news are a large army."

●What do you appreciate about how Jehovah and Jesus dignify women?

Jehovah values spirituality above gender, and Jesus taught and honored women in a culture that often didn't.

●How can sisters expand their ministry in the last days?

Through the vast preaching and teaching field, and by mentoring others (Titus 2) — a lifesaving work.
