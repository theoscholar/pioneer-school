Participate in Various Forms of Our Ministry

Opening Discussion · every opportunity

Heart of it: House to house is our main method — but the first-century Christians grabbed every chance to preach: publicly, informally, and in homes. They focused on people, not houses.

Jesus foretold a worldwide preaching work for the last days, and it's speeding up with pioneers at the forefront. When it's hard to find people at home, we follow the apostles' example and reach people wherever they are.

Mark 13:10 — "Also, in all the nations, the good news has to be preached first."

🔍Look it up: Matt. 24:14·Acts 20:20, 21

Informal Witnessing · conversations, not sermons

Heart of it: Informal witnessing is just friendly conversation. You don't have to open with a scripture — aim first to start a warm chat, and the good news can come in naturally later.

QHow did Jesus witness informally to the Samaritan woman?

He started with common ground (a drink of water), showed genuine interest, and gently steered the talk to spiritual things — warm, natural, unhurried.

🔍Look it up: John 4:7-26

QWhat's the natural result when truth fills our heart?

We speak up — "out of the heart's abundance the mouth speaks." Meditating on Jehovah's goodness and people's real needs moves us to share.

Luke 6:45 — "Out of the heart's abundance his mouth speaks."

🔍Look it up: Ps. 40:5·Matt. 13:52

QHow do tact, humility, and empathy improve our conversations?

Gracious, "seasoned with salt" speech, a humble manner, and empathy make our message appealing — and help us know when to speak and when to stop.

🔍Look it up: Col. 4:6·Phil. 2:3·1 Cor. 9:19-23

Public Witnessing · reaching the public square

Heart of it: Preaching in public places — carts, transport, parks, business territory — reaches people we'd never find at home. It's a normal, valued part of our ministry, not an "alternative."

QWhat do Acts 16:13 and 17:17 teach about appropriate occasions to witness?

Paul went where the people gathered — a riverside, the marketplace, the synagogue. We do the same: carts, public transport, parks, streets, shopping areas, and business territory.

🔍Look it up: Acts 16:13·Acts 17:17·Matt. 7:12

Maintain Boldness · pray, then act

Heart of it: Boldness isn't fearlessness — it's speaking up despite fear, backed by prayer. Even a shy child can do it with a "Ready, set, go!"

QHow do we show boldness, and what role does prayer play?

We speak "with boldness" as Paul did, having first prayed for the right words. Then we work in harmony with our prayers — carrying literature and looking for openings.

🔍Look it up: 1 Thess. 2:2·Eph. 6:19

Say it your way: Think of one everyday setting — work, travel, errands — where you could start a friendly conversation. What might you say?

Your quick prep plan ✅

  • 3 comments ready: a natural informal-witnessing opener, a public-witnessing setting, and how prayer builds boldness.
  • 1 scripture to read aloud: Mark 13:10 or Luke 6:45.
  • An experience: have one ready about an informal or public witnessing opportunity you took.
